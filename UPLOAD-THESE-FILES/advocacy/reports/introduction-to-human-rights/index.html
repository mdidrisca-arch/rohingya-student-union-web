Skills for Advocacy · Session 8/12 · Human Rights

Introduction to Human Rights

Training Title
Introduction to Human Rights
Trainer
Nezam Uddin (Lawyer and Legal Professional)
Organized by
Rohingya Student Union (RSU)
Date
27th April
Session
8 of 12 — Skills for Advocacy

Overview

The Rohingya Student Union successfully conducted the 8th session of the Skills for Advocacy training focused on Human Rights. Facilitated by Mr. Nezam Uddin, this training aimed to provide in-depth legal information to the participants with essential skills related to Human Rights. Participants actively engaged in discussions and interactive activities, gaining valuable insights and practical knowledge on how to effectively engage with communities for advocacy purposes.

Key Topics Covered

  • Understanding of Human Rights and Fundamental Rights
  • Overview of the Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR)
  • 1951 International Refugee Convention and 1967 Additional Protocol
  • International Covenant on Economic, Social, and Cultural Rights (ICESCR)
  • International Covenant on Civil and Political Rights (ICCPR)
  • Convention on the Elimination of all Forms of Discrimination Against Women (CEDAW)
  • Convention on the Rights of the Child (CRC)

Acknowledgement

We extend our sincere appreciation to our trainer, Mr. Nezam Uddin, for his informative facilitation, as well as to all the participants for their enthusiastic involvement and dedication throughout the training session.
