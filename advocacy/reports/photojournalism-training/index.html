Skills for Advocacy · Session 6/12 · Photojournalism

Photojournalism Training

Training6/12 Comprehensive Skills for Advocacy
Date07/04/2024
TopicPhotojournalism
TrainerAbdullah (Khin Maung Thein)
Organized byRohingya Student Union (RSU)

Overview

The Rohingya Student Union conducted a training session on Photojournalism led by our award-winning photographer Abdullah. Participants learned about the fundamentals of photojournalism, photography techniques, and writing effective photo captions. Mr. Abdullah provided valuable insights and practical tips to enhance participants' advocacy skills through visual storytelling.

Topics Covered

  • What is Photojournalism
  • Photography Techniques
  • Writing Photo Captions
  • Visual storytelling for human rights advocacy
  • Using images to communicate social issues effectively

Outcome

The session was engaging and informative, empowering participants to effectively communicate social issues through compelling images. We thank Abdullah for his expertise and look forward to seeing the impact of visual storytelling in advocacy efforts by RSU members.
