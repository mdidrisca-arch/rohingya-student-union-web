Train-the-Teacher Workshop on Educational Literacy

Trainer
Engr. Muhammad Furquan Mirza
Venue
TİKA Computer and Robotics Lab
Organized by
Rohingya Student Union (RSU)

Overview

The Rohingya Student Union (RSU) successfully conducted its first Train-the-Teacher Workshop on educational literacy, aimed at bridging the education gap for every child while prioritizing girls' access to learning. The session focused on modern teaching methods, creating an inclusive and effective classroom environment, and ensuring strong safeguarding measures for girls and all students.

Session Focus

  • Modern teaching methods for diverse classroom settings
  • Creating inclusive and effective learning environments
  • Ensuring strong safeguarding measures for girls and all students
  • Bridging the education gap for every child in the community
  • Practical teaching tools and interactive learning strategies

Impact

The training was led by Engr. Muhammad Furquan Mirza and held at the TİKA Computer and Robotics Lab, providing teachers with hands-on skills to transform their classrooms into safe, inclusive, and effective spaces for every learner.
