Training Title
DRM Training for Rohingya Women and Girls
Organized by
Rohingya Student Union (RSU)
Implemented by
Team Balukhali
Focus
Women & Girls' Leadership in Disaster Preparedness

Overview

RSU successfully conducted a two-day Disaster Risk Management (DRM) Training dedicated to Rohingya women and girls, reaffirming its commitment to inclusive community development and disaster resilience. As primary caregivers and often the first responders within their households, women and girls play a vital role in reducing disaster risk and protecting families — this training equipped them with practical skills, life-saving knowledge, and the confidence to lead during emergencies.

Training Topics

  • Fundamentals and key concepts of Disaster Risk Management
  • Understanding the disaster risk profile of the refugee camps
  • Household and community-level disaster preparedness planning
  • Disaster risk reduction, prevention, and mitigation strategies
  • Public awareness and community engagement for preparedness
  • Hazard identification, early warning systems, and emergency communication
  • Basic Medical and Psychological First Aid
  • Analysis of real-life disaster case studies

Acknowledgement

RSU extends its sincere appreciation to every participant for their enthusiasm and active engagement, and acknowledges the dedication of its trainers in creating a supportive, empowering learning environment. Implemented by Team Balukhali, this training reflects RSU's vision of building resilient, inclusive communities where women and girls are empowered as leaders in disaster preparedness.
