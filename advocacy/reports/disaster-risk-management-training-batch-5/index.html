Training Title
Disaster Risk Management Training — Batch 5
Date
9–10 December 2023
Organized by
Rohingya Student Union (RSU)

Overview

RSU successfully concluded the fifth batch of its two-day Disaster Risk Management (DRM) Training on 9–10 December 2023, continuing its commitment to strengthening disaster preparedness and building resilient communities across the Rohingya refugee camps. The workshop provided both theoretical understanding and practical experience through an engaging, action-oriented learning environment facilitated by experienced trainers.

Training Highlights

  • Interactive lectures on Disaster Risk Management principles
  • Hands-on practical exercises to reinforce learning
  • Emergency response drills and disaster preparedness simulations
  • Group presentations and collaborative learning activities
  • Constructive feedback sessions to strengthen skills and confidence

Acknowledgement

RSU extends its sincere gratitude to every participant whose enthusiasm and commitment made the training a success, and to the dedicated trainers whose expertise ensured an engaging and impactful learning experience. The successful completion of this fifth DRM Training marks another milestone in RSU's ongoing mission to empower the Rohingya community with life-saving knowledge and practical skills.
