Final-Term Teacher Development Workshop

Program
Literacy for Rohingya Girls
Workshop
Final-Term Teacher Development Workshop
Organized by
Rohingya Student Union (RSU)

Overview

The Final-Term Teacher Development Workshop for the Literacy for Rohingya Girls program brought educators together to reflect on their teaching journeys, celebrate progress, and prepare to make learning more effective for Rohingya girls.

Workshop Highlights

  • Reviewed students’ mid-term progress
  • Identified learning gaps and shared classroom experiences
  • Developed practical strategies to strengthen reading and writing
  • Explored ways to improve speaking, listening, and mathematics instruction

Moving Forward

Together, the teachers renewed their commitment to helping every child receive quality education and achieve the program’s learning goals.
