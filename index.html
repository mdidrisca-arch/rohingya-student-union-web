Rohingya Student Union

Empowering Rohingya youth and women through education, leadership, advocacy, and community engagement. We strive to amplify their voices, unlock their potential, and create meaningful opportunities to build a stronger, more inclusive, and resilient Rohingya community with a brighter future for all.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide every step we take.

Leadership Development

Cultivating the next generation of Rohingya leaders through mentorship, training, and hands-on experience in organizing and decision-making.

Human Rights & Advocacy

Championing the rights of Rohingya students and communities through education, awareness campaigns, and engagement with international human rights frameworks.

Education & Empowerment

Expanding access to quality education and skill-building so every Rohingya student can pursue their potential with confidence and purpose.
